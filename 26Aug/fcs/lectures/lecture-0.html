Course promise

We will open the black box

You already know how to write programs. This course asks what must exist beneath a program for it to run.

Softwareprograms and instructions
Machine architectureCPU, memory, and control
Digital circuitsarithmetic, storage, and data movement
Logic gatessimple rules made from switches

We will keep asking the same question at each level: what does this layer need from the layer below?

Learning outcomes

By the end of the course, you should be able to...

01

Explain how logic gates grow into arithmetic, memory, and a stored-program computer.

02

Design, implement, simulate, and test hardware components using HDL.

03

Reason about machine instructions, data paths, control, and memory.

04

Connect this machine to ideas you will later meet in operating systems and compilers.

Collaboration and integrity

Discuss ideas. Submit your own work.

Permitted in labs
  • Discussing concepts and approaches
  • Asking classmates and staff questions
  • Explaining why a test fails
Not permitted
  • Copying another student's implementation
  • Plagiarism in labs or projects
  • Copying or unauthorized collaboration in examinations

Collaboration should improve your understanding, not replace your thinking.
